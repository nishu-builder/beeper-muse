01 / The idea

Keep the conversation.
Change where you have it.

Send a message in Beeper. The extension passes it to your open Muse tab and brings the reply back. It gets its own chat, with you and your assistant on the right sides.

More than plain text

Formatted replies, images, edits, reactions, and typing indicators. Support is still being refined.

Just Chrome running

The extension handles the Beeper connection. No separate daemon, server, or companion app.

A little catch-up

Bring over recent messages already loaded in Muse, or start with new messages only.

Open all the way down

MIT licensed, with the code, architecture, and known issues available on GitHub.

03 / A few loose ends

Good to know.

Does everything in Muse work here?

Not yet. Chat and images are supported, but image uploads are still a preview. Approvals, interactive cards, and browser controls stay in Muse. A hidden Muse tab may defer image loading; opening it lets the image finish syncing. See what’s verified and what’s still open.

Does Chrome need to stay open?

Yes. Your computer’s Chrome extension is the bridge, even when you use Beeper on your phone. Keep the connected Muse tab and the extension’s connection tab open, and leave Muse’s message box empty.

Where does my data go?

The extension connects your signed-in Muse tab to Beeper. Registration, encryption keys, and sync state stay in your Chrome profile; bridged messages and media travel to the two services. There is no additional Beeper Muse server. This site has no analytics or third-party scripts. Read the privacy notes.

Is this an official integration?

No. This is an independent, community-built project, unaffiliated with Beeper or Muse. It uses Beeper’s bridge protocol and observes the selected Muse webpage. Read how it works.
